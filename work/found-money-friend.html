Open the live HVAC demo

Businesses spend enormous time finding the next customer. Meanwhile, revenue from customers they’ve already found quietly disappears — an estimate nobody followed up on, a patient overdue for treatment, a checkout that walked away.

Revenue Bloom is a self-improving AI agent that finds those missed opportunities, recommends the next best action, acts with human approval, measures the result, and learns. Not another dashboard. An AI employee with initiative.

FIND → RECOMMEND → ACT → LEARN → FIND AGAIN

The demo

We built it around a fictional premium HVAC company, Northgate. The agent finds thousands of dollars sitting in forgotten estimates and recommends a recovery campaign — with a discount.

The owner: We don’t discount. We’re a premium company.

Revenue Bloom changes the recommendation, asks if it should remember that, and does. After the campaign, it finds another $6,700 opportunity. This time the recommendation already uses a premium service benefit. The feedback didn’t just get stored. It changed what the AI did next.
